Learn English

Learn English is a YouTube channel for English learners. Two hosts, Mia and Leo, teach real, everyday English through calm podcast-style conversations with subtitles.

What we make

  • Business English · English at Work: introducing yourself, emails, meetings, talking to your manager.
  • Real English Conversations: B1 podcast episodes about everyday situations.
  • Everyday English A2: the café, the restaurant, the train station, the bus and work.
  • Slow English for Sleep: calm bedtime stories with slowly repeated phrases.

Every video includes word-by-word subtitles, key phrases and a short quiz. The hosts' voices are AI-generated. The scripts and lessons are written for the channel.
