Privacy Policy: Learn English Uploader

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the Learn English Uploader (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the Learn English YouTube channel.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner, to manage their own YouTube channel. It isn't offered to the public, and other people can't sign in to it.

2. What data the App accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the App with their Google account, the App uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel and schedule when they are published;
  • set video thumbnails and upload subtitle (caption) files;
  • post a comment on the owner's own videos and add videos to the owner's playlists;
  • read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is the right channel.

The App doesn't collect, store or process personal data of viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users.

3. How data is stored

The OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret and refresh token) are stored privately in the owner's own working environment and are used only to perform the actions above. They aren't shared, sold or given to any third party.

4. Sharing

The App doesn't sell, rent or share any data with third parties. It doesn't use data for advertising.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The App also uses YouTube API Services, so it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the App's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Select Learn English Uploader and choose Remove access.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
